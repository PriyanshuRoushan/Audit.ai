# User Research Notes

Formal interviews have not yet been completed during the MVP stage.

However, product direction was influenced by observing:
- Reddit discussions
- startup communities
- AI engineering conversations
- Product Hunt launches

## Common Pain Points

### Startup Founders
- Using too many overlapping AI tools
- No visibility into AI spending

### Engineering Managers
- Redundant subscriptions
- Unclear team seat allocation

### Indie Hackers
- Confusion between AI tools
- Difficulty choosing the right model

## Product Impact

This research influenced:
- recommendation logic
- neutral optimization positioning
- operational efficiency focus
